personal project · mar 2026

research
pivotrl: pivot-weighted reasoning distillation
personal research · ongoing 2026
  • not all tokens in a reasoning trace are equally worth learning from. i detect high-loss pivot points in Kimi K2.5 traces and up-weight them when distilling into Gemma 4.
  • uniform SFT on reasoning traces dropped GSM8K from 85.5% to 74.5%. pivot-weighted SFT recovered it to 80%. the null result is the point: naive distillation on reasoning traces actively hurts, which isn't obvious from the literature.
rppg with adaptive roi selection
masters thesis · sf state · 2025–2026
  • rPPG extracts heart rate from video via subtle color changes in facial skin. most methods fix the region of interest to the full face or a static patch, but signal quality varies heavily by lighting, motion, and skin tone across regions.
  • trained a PPO agent to dynamically select and weight facial regions per frame using a 64-dim state from MediaPipe. policy improved HR_MAE from 33.58 to 21.76 BPM. the honest catch: gains came at a cost to PPG waveform correlation, and dense rewards underperformed sparse ones. the waveform signal is harder to translate into useful gradients than i expected.

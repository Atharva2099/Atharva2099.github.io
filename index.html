PROJECTS

01

01cross-lingual representation geometry and layerwise probing↗

mechanistic interpretability · 2026
  • i wanted to explore whether models have a shared representation for political ideas across different architectures, languages, and layers.
  • curated a multilingual dataset from Anthropic's llm_global_opinions, filtered 2,556 questions down to 580 suitable for probing, converted them into labelled statements, and translated them into English, Hindi, Marathi, German, Spanish, and Mandarin using Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite.
  • ran five models from different families on these statements, captured residual stream activations across layers, and trained linear probes. best within-language accuracies ranged from 81.03% to 86.55%, usually peaking around the middle layers depending on the model.
  • trained probes in one language and tested them on the other five: average cross-language accuracy was 68.89% for OLMo 3, 67.72% for Ministral, 81.45% for Gemma 2, and 78.24% for Qwen 3.5. Gemma and Qwen showed much stronger transfer than OLMo and Ministral—representations appear to be shared across languages, but the alignment varies significantly across architectures.

02wolfeclick: pokémon rl with grpo↗

rl for games · mar 2026
  • i wanted to train LLMs for long-horizon reasoning under hidden information. Pokémon fits: you don't see the full opponent team, and a decision on turn two still matters at turn fifteen.

04

04pivotrl: pivot-weighted reasoning distillation↗

reasoning post-training · ongoing 2026
  • not all tokens in a reasoning trace are equally worth learning from. i detect high-loss pivot points in Kimi K2.5 traces and up-weight them when distilling into Gemma 4.
  • uniform SFT on reasoning traces dropped GSM8K from 85.5% to 74.5%. pivot-weighted SFT recovered it to 80%. the null result is the point: naive distillation on reasoning traces actively hurts, which isn't obvious from the literature.
